# PlayTown Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 10, 2026

PlayTown is a sports community platform designed to help athletes, teams, clubs, and tournament organizers connect, participate in sporting activities, and share achievements.

This Privacy Policy explains how PlayTown collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, PlayTown may collect:

- **Account information:** Name, email address, and information required for authentication.
- **Athlete profiles:** Profile photographs, sports interests, teams, clubs, achievements, and other information you provide.
- **Community content:** Posts, photographs, comments, likes, match details, and tournament participation.
- **Location information:** Location information used to help users discover nearby sporting activities, where permission is provided.
- **Technical information:** Device and diagnostic information needed to maintain application security, performance, and reliability, where collected.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

- Create and manage PlayTown accounts.
- Provide athlete profiles and sports community features.
- Help users discover players, clubs, matches, and tournaments.
- Display rankings, achievements, and sporting activities.
- Deliver relevant account and activity notifications.
- Maintain application security and improve performance.

## 3. Public Information

Profile information, sports achievements, posts, and other content may be visible to other PlayTown users depending on the features and privacy settings available in the application.

Users should avoid publishing sensitive personal information in public content.

## 4. Data Storage and Service Providers

PlayTown uses third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate its services, including cloud database and media storage services.

These may include MongoDB Atlas for application data and Cloudflare R2 for media storage.

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers to deliver and maintain PlayTown's functionality.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and loss.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, comply with applicable legal obligations, and resolve legitimate disputes.

Users may request account deletion and deletion of associated personal information by contacting us using the email address below.

Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and compliance purposes.

## 7. Children's Privacy

PlayTown is committed to protecting children's privacy. Age eligibility, parental consent requirements, and any applicable protections will be implemented in accordance with relevant laws and platform requirements.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to PlayTown's services, data practices, or legal requirements.

The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
